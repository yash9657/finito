# FinFlow redesign

Public `/`: Persuade. Authenticated workspace: Operate.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Show a clear path from source record to finance decision. A working sample, source evidence, and review state carry the pitch.

OWN-WORLD: User-selected dark institutional direction: graphite surfaces, cool white Geist typography, tabular financial figures, fine gray rules, restrained sage actions, amber exceptions. No decorative gradients, generated illustration, customer logo claims, or chat sparkle motifs.

STORY: The visitor sees reconciliation, invoice review, and variance analysis; understands calculations and approvals; enters the workspace with CSV or Excel sources.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A 1240px content grid, slim branded navigation, large left-aligned thesis, supporting copy and a right-aligned CTA, followed by a wide sample workpaper with a table and evidence detail. Narrow screens stack the CTA and evidence and provide local table scrolling.

FORM: Code-led, typography and interactive financial workpaper. The earlier seed 5cd6faac has no retained roll artifact and is unverified. The user-selected institutional direction is the authority; no comp fidelity or verified seed claim is made. Signature interaction: keyboard-accessible sample tabs update the evidence and result together. Motion is restrained and respects reduced-motion settings.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
