# FinFlow

## Platform
Web application: Next.js App Router, React, Supabase.

## Product truth
FinFlow helps finance teams reconcile ERP exports against bank statements, match invoices to purchase orders, prepare budget-versus-actual commentary, and investigate uploaded financial datasets. Deterministic code calculates money; models assist with matching and narrative; people review consequential actions and final reports. Finance workflows use validated CSV and Excel inputs. The general data library supports delimited files, multi-sheet workbooks, JSON/JSONL, text/Markdown and embedded-text PDFs. Investigations can inspect sources and run bounded JavaScript calculations with exact-decimal helpers; generated analyses expose their source coverage and method for review. Reports and authorization history can be exported. No live banking, ERP synchronization, payment execution, certification, or enterprise scale claim is established.

## Memory and continuity
Original files, conversations, workflow records and private activity history remain in storage. The agent retrieves compact relevant excerpts and original-record pointers instead of replaying the archive. Personal preferences are editable and removable, and automatic recall can be disabled. Indexed retrieval includes dates and original-row citations, but semantic paraphrase recall and instant answers are not guaranteed. Activity capture covers durable actions and best-effort page visits, excluding drafts, keystrokes and credentials.
